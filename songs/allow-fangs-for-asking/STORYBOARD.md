## "Allow" evolution
Frost on the outside of her glass (his) → light reflected in the glass (chorus 1) → the button on her phone (chorus 2) →
her own fingertip in the sister's fog, backwards (break) → the sister's phone (final) → a town of lit windows (outro).

## Easter eggs
Permissions climb Notifications → Location → Camera (green dot) → Microphone (orange dot) → Contacts → the sister.
Bite marks are notification badges. Phone brightness is the inverse of the candle all film long. Her reflection fades in
the glass during verse 2 before the mirror makes it explicit. The bolt is never broken. The sister's prompt comes from
"Sis". The final windows are phone-screen blue.
