# Brand photo

Every thumbnail is a brand cover ([`studio/brand.py`](../../studio/brand.py)), and its lockup chip in the top-left
corner shows a photo in a green ring next to the name and "</> CLAUDE ANIMATED THIS". The photo isn't in the repo.
Put a square one here as `aditazzz.jpg` (or point `BRAND_PHOTO` at any image, and change `NAME` in `studio/brand.py`
for your own name). Without it the chip shows a plain dark disc in the ring.
